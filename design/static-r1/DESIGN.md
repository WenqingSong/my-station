# R1 — editorial portfolio

## Composition

Hero → SurgeCart → Offer 捕手 → Agent Workflow → About → Contact.

Hero uses the role as typographic focus, with Arthur as the signature. The frozen pointer field occupies the right side and loses contrast on narrow screens. Three compact project links form the handoff to Work; no introduction or skills section repeats the same content.

SurgeCart is the main piece: one full-width blue transaction study, followed by a problem statement, decisions, checks, and boundary. Offer uses an asymmetric document composition. Workflow is a quieter editorial/tooling section, not another colored card. About switches to pale olive paper and a small off-axis portrait; internship and interests form the lower part of the spread. Contact is the only full dark section.

## Type and rhythm

- Latin: Instrument Sans; Chinese: Noto Sans SC, re-subset to cover all of this study's copy, with PingFang SC / Microsoft YaHei fallback. No faux bold or italic.
- Instrument was selected for the open, less compressed word shapes and a calmer mixed-script texture. Compare with Inter using the same words and sizing at `?specimen=1`; final judgment is checked visually, not from font names alone.
- Display: 80–162px desktop / viewport-scaled mobile; weight 500; tracking roughly −0.065em; line height ~1.
- Section titles: 30–46px; About has one wider Latin headline, 42–84px.
- Project titles: 48–82px for the flagship; smaller for supporting work.
- Main Chinese body: 16–17px desktop / 14px phone; secondary notes smaller; line height 1.85–1.95. Do not apply Latin negative tracking to paragraph text.
- Captions are secondary only; factual explanations remain in body type outside illustrations.
- Side margins: 23px on common phones, scaling toward 96px desktop. Content caps at 1600px.
- Major gaps: 78–152px; supporting paragraphs and rows use smaller, consistent spacing.

## Palette

Paper `#f0eee7`; ink `#232520`; secondary `#696b63`; accent `#304fe5`; About `#e4e5dc`; Contact `#232722`.

No gradients, glass panels, glow, noisy cursor trails, or ornamental badges. Borders are used for content grouping and alignment. Code-native project visuals add content, not decorative motion.

## Narrow screens

- Hero retains role, introduction and three separate tap targets; the circular link keeps its accessible name.
- Full-width case cover is recomposed vertically, not scaled as a complete desktop screenshot.
- Offer text precedes its diagram; Workflow becomes a compact linear story.
- Portrait moves above the narrative and stays around two thirds width, rather than becoming a full-screen face.
- Contact details stack; mail and phone retain native actions.

## Explicitly deferred

Opening and all scroll/pointer choreography; verified evidence/repository/live-product entrances; real Offer screenshot integration; mail-copy feedback; full performance/accessibility certification; production deployment. This deliverable is static composition acceptance, not a claim of finished award-level design.
